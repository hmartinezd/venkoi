# Venkoi Brand Voice

## Voice

Venkoi sounds concrete, plainspoken, specific, and operational. Write with confidence, but without hype. Explain what happens, what connects, what a person reviews, and what decision becomes easier. Prefer evidence and product behavior over adjectives.

If a sentence could appear unchanged on almost any software website, make it more specific or remove it.

## Company positioning

Venkoi is a product-first software company. Its own focused products come first; selected mobile and web projects are secondary. The featured restaurant product is the current commercial focus, but Venkoi is not a restaurant-only company.

## Product writing rules

- Explain the workflow, not a disconnected feature list.
- State limitations where they affect trust or a buying decision.
- Never invent functionality, precision, savings, customers, timelines, or proof.
- Invoice detection suggests; the restaurant reviews, corrects, and explicitly posts.
- Show missing cost coverage instead of implying false precision.
- Keep zero distinct from uncounted.
- Reorder support prepares a purchase list; it does not electronically place supplier orders.
- Preserve Android, designed-to-function-locally, and initial single-location truth.
- Use `{productName}` or `FEATURED_PRODUCT.name` in public copy rather than hard-coding the temporary product name.

## CTA hierarchy

- Product interest → Demo / Access Request
- Custom mobile or web work → Contact

Do not merge these paths: Demo ≠ Access Request. Technical program identifiers may use `early-access`, but public copy should normally describe the action plainly as requesting or applying for access. An access request is an application; do not imply that acceptance or timing is guaranteed.

## English

Write short sentences with concrete nouns and verbs.

- Do: “Capture the invoice, review what was detected, then post the purchase when it is correct.”
- Don’t: “Streamline invoice processing with intelligent automation.”
- Do: “If a source cost is missing, the product shows the gap.”
- Don’t: “Unlock powerful cost insights.”
- Do: “We build our own products first. We also take on selected mobile and web projects.”
- Don’t: “We empower businesses with transformative software solutions.”

## Spanish

Rewrite the meaning naturally in Spanish; do not mechanically copy English sentence structure. Prefer terms such as “diseñado para funcionar localmente,” “primera versión,” “versión inicial,” and “solicitar acceso.” Use the site's direct, informal voice consistently. Avoid unnecessary English terminology, especially “software local-first” and “flujo de lanzamiento.” EN and ES must agree on product truth, limitations, availability, access qualification, and CTA intent even when their sentence structures differ.

## Avoid

Avoid stock phrases including: “unlock powerful insights,” “streamline your operations,” “transform your workflow,” “built for modern teams,” “seamless end-to-end experience,” “empower your business,” “turn complexity into clarity,” “revolutionize,” “game-changing,” “all-in-one solution,” “supercharge,” “next-generation,” “future-proof,” “single source of truth,” “drive efficiency,” and “maximize productivity.”

Use vague adjectives such as “powerful,” “innovative,” “robust,” “intuitive,” “smart,” “modern,” “seamless,” and “comprehensive” only when the sentence proves the claim.
